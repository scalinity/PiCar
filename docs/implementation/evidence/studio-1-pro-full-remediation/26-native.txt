Observations (not F1-F11):
- On macOS a right-button pan that starts over empty space clears the selection: contextmenu fires on right-button down, which
  React Three Fiber reports as a missed click with no movement. Present in Studio 1 as well; recorded, not fixed.
- "Resume guided view" restarting playback was suspected once and did not reproduce in two controlled attempts (guided and manual
  camera, from the end of a step): the timeline stays put.
- Window placement: every launch opened the window partly off-screen (1440 x 900 configured on a 1168 x 733 work area). Fixed
  at the owner's request in commit 7991eb2; two later launches opened at (23, 36) 1121 x 704, centred. native-01 predates that fix.
