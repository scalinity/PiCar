Found and fixed during this pass (both verified in the final bundle and covered by a browser test):
- 3D picking: a click on a part selected a tray tile instead. Tile edges are LineLoops, which three.js raycasts within one
  world unit (a metre here); the edges are now excluded from picking (resources.ts).
- A click that only selected a part switched the camera to manual. A press now yields the guided view once it moves the
  camera, or when it interrupts a guided move (StudioScene.tsx).

Environment finding (not an app defect): during an earlier pass the Terminal window, which screenshots hide from computer
use, stood in front of the app and received clicks aimed at the dock, which produced selections and camera changes the
script did not intend. The window list confirmed Terminal frontmost over the app. The final pass ran in native fullscreen
on its own Space, and a clean replay of the same sequence behaved as designed.
