# Owner policy: independent engineering and instructional fidelity tracks

Effective owner amendment: 2026-09-30. Scope: purchased M5 components, and a conditional M6 handoff only. Original M0–M4 authority, canonical records and strict G-COMPONENT/G-CAD/G-GEOMETRY gates remain intact. This additive policy supersedes their prohibition on *presentation-only* purchased proxies only within the new instructional track. It does not weaken any engineering admission condition or release a runtime pack.

The PiCar-X project has two fidelity tracks. Engineering admission requires the original audited evidence and independent CAD checks. Instructional admission requires visual/tutorial fidelity sufficient to guide assembly without materially misleading the user. `INSTRUCTIONAL_ADMITTED` never means `ENGINEERING_ADMITTED`, physical equality, manufacturing accuracy or safety certification.

## Persistent contracts

The machine-readable policy and closed receipt schema are under `digital-twin/validation/expected/m5/`. Their validator requires the track tag, source/blocker closure, exact protected facts, isolated presentation parameters and artifact provenance. They are outside canonical model/evidence/schema preimages. The per-definition dispositions cover exactly the fifty purchased definitions plus six owner-bound integral leads that add zero purchased items. Original strict M5 receipts remain unchanged.

Engineering status is `ENGINEERING_ADMITTED` only with an independently verified strict G-COMPONENT receipt for its declared scope; otherwise `PARTIAL` or `BLOCKED`. Instructional status is `INSTRUCTIONAL_ADMITTED` or `BLOCKED`. A partial source investigation does not change its blocked strict gate. The new `G-INSTRUCTIONAL-COMPONENT` passes only with complete honest coverage and zero tutorial-critical blocked definitions. Blocked components must never be hidden to manufacture a pass.

## Evidence firewall

Canonical Measurement, Claim, EvidenceRecord, DerivationRecord and MechanicalInterface semantics remain unchanged. Unknown engineering values retain their unresolved tagged values without numbers. Presentation parameters carry value, unit, basis, source refs, approximation kind, visual purpose, a mandatory non-engineering flag and engineering blocker refs. They are not canonical measurements or VERIFIED/DERIVED engineering claims. No approximation resolves a Q blocker or an open conflict.

Instructional artifacts and their numeric parameters must be refused at engineering measurement/interface/route/solver and G-COMPONENT/G-CAD/G-GEOMETRY inputs. A caller cannot gain authority by changing a status label: engineering admission requires the original independent gate proof and exact source/artifact binding. Future solvers and exporters must apply this guard before accepting inputs. No new engineering solver or runtime loader is implemented by M5.

Allowed purposes are recognition, picking/tray views, tutorial orientation, rough external appearance/envelopes, exploded/animation presentation, future visual placement, schematic flexible cables and cosmetic detail. Forbidden authority includes mating/fit/interference proof, torque, engagement, fabrication, safety clearances, voltages, exact bend limits, collision guarantees and exact mechanical axes. A presentation origin/anchor is replaceable and explicitly not a mechanical datum.

Protected facts may not be approximated: IDs, component counts and roles, variants, handedness, step/instance use, printed fastener designation, connector labels/endpoints, servo and motor roles, ribbon variants, temporary/final wiring and P11 zeroing, Plate E-right/F-left, conflicts, provenance, observed stock, unproved OEM identity, voltage and battery safety. Known source-supported critical features must be represented correctly; unknown critical engineering geometry stays unresolved.

## Representation and source rules

Prefer applicable official CAD/drawings, existing private component imagery, applicable official product imagery/docs, V40 printed panels, then explicitly limited family/generic sources. Similarity does not prove OEM identity. Private imagery remains ignored, local, unstaged and absent from public/runtime assets. Photos without a reproducible calibration/uncertainty derivation supply appearance/orientation only. Rights are source-specific; newly authored analytic proxies do not publish source images/CAD. Unresolved source redistribution remains blocked.

Printed metric diameter/length may drive nominal instructional screw/standoff forms without inferring head/drive/pitch/material/thread class/depth/torque. Washers A/B and every opaque rivet code remain distinct; rivets retain owned body/pin subnodes with one inventory item. Do not decode rivet codes into engineering dimensions. Pan/tilt/steering servos and horns stay separate without SG90/MG90S substitution. Motors retain left/right, rear-drive and integral lead semantics. Front wheels preserve S26 washer/rivet use; rear wheels preserve S27 shaft use.

HAT revision and hidden pin maps stay unresolved; show only accepted labels and source-supported connector groups. Camera visual envelope must identify its open conflict, never an average. Ultrasonic geometry must not resolve 5V versus V40 3V3. Pi sources retain their approximation/guidance limits and do not identify actual revision/header/cooler configuration. Battery is an external tutorial envelope with no safety claim. Cables can be zero-solid endpoint schematics; consumables can be zero-solid abstractions with stock/allocation semantics intact.

Cosmetic curvature, fillets, head proportions, tread, casing/component details, cable slack/curvature/thickness, external envelopes and presentation anchor offsets may be approximated only with declared basis and purpose. Every approximation and omission is reviewable. Generation repeatability and BRep validity certify the authored representation, not physical dimensional accuracy. No M4 planar mesh proof applies to curved proxies; M5 creates no mesh.

## Closure and future work

Fresh M4 tooling qualification, actual BRep checks, source/receipt closure, per-definition semantic checks, mandatory positive/negative firewall tests and two clean runs support the instructional gate. Canonical upstream hashes and old failure history must remain intact. A local instructional acceptance commit is permitted only when the new gate passes and upstream integrity holds. No strict M5 acceptance SHA follows from it. Rollback is additive and affects only named presentation revisions/dependencies.

M6 remains unimplemented here. Owner instructional M6 authorization requires instructional M5 PASS, its local acceptance commit and integrity PASS for the original private plate ZIP/manifest/images. The handoff must carry strict engineering blockers, A–H counts, owner-reviewed probable thickness/handedness, uncertainty requirements and separate instructional plate admission. No hardware, owner session data, source publication, remote writes or M7+ work is authorized by this policy.
