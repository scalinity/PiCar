# PiCar project instructions

Personal PiCar-X (SunFounder Z0104V40) companion and digital twin. The app is `picarx-companion/` (React, Tauri); the twin and its CAD pipeline are `digital-twin/`; the audited specification is `docs/digital-twin/`; milestone records are `docs/implementation/`. Assembly Studio is planned in `docs/digital-twin/ASSEMBLY_STUDIO_PLAN.md` and built as described in `docs/digital-twin/ASSEMBLY_STUDIO_PIPELINE.md`.

## Geometry fidelity bars

These apply to every part definition, every revision and every Studio pack. The owner builds the physical car next to the Studio, so a part that looks wrong teaches the wrong thing.

1. **Symmetric parts are symmetric by construction.** If a part is a mirror image of itself in the real kit (Plate A's arms, its deck outline, paired flanges), model one half and mirror it; never trace the two sides separately. Each such definition carries a test that measures the mirror deviation of its outline: aim for 0.05 mm or less, and treat anything over 0.2 mm as a defect. Genuinely handed parts keep their handedness (Plate E is right, Plate F is left; protected facts are never "symmetrized").
2. **Curves are real curves.** Rounded ends, fillets, holes and slots are true arcs and circles in the CAD: lines with tangent arcs, concentric with the hole they surround where the photographs show that. A traced polyline is never the final outline. The Studio tessellation keeps chord error at or below 0.02 mm and the angle at or below 0.15 rad, so no curve shows facets at Studio zoom.
3. **Detail comes from the best documentation available.** Where a part has published mechanical documentation (the Raspberry Pi 5 and Zero 2 W drawings and product briefs, SunFounder module pages), model every externally visible feature it documents (connectors, headers with pins, chips, buttons, LEDs, mounting features) at the drawn position and size. Kit parts without such documentation take their detail from the owner's photographs, scaled by the card or coin in frame. A box-and-cylinder proxy is a labelled stopgap that gets replaced, never a finished part.
4. **Cross-check against every available image, often.** Before adopting a geometry revision, compare it with every relevant reference: the owner's photographs, the V40 manual panels and official drawings. Render the matching view, overlay or measure the silhouette using the photo's scale reference, and record the measured deviation in the revision's review record. Repeat the check after each change and again before calling the work done. A part is not done while any reference contradicts it.
5. **Fix geometry at the source.** Fidelity changes are additive instructional revisions through the existing resolver (a new artifact, old and new hashes, a review record). Then rerun the M7 chain and rebuild the Studio pack. Blender only applies materials, lights and cameras; it never remodels, moves or smooths geometry.
   - Where M7 consumes a definition structurally (the Pi 5 and Zero 2 W boards, whose solids M7 locates by order), detail is added as a **registered display model** instead (`digital-twin/cad/twin_cad/fidelity/`): authored CAD in the same part frame, with every mounting feature coincident. Its record carries a relation check against the instructional artifact, an overlap check in every closure that places it, and a vendor cross-check where vendor CAD exists. The Studio states both the shape shown and the shape checked, and it shows every display overlap rather than hiding it. Adopting the detail as the instructional artifact is M7 work.
   - Exports must be byte-stable: never chamfer or fillet through edge selectors that follow OCC hash order. Build profiles explicitly, or intersect with revolved profiles.
6. **Assembly datums stay put for looks.** Holes, slots and mounting features that step closures use keep their positions. Move one only when evidence shows it is wrong, as a separate correction, and revalidate the affected steps. Never move one to make a render look better or to clear an overlap.
7. **Licences and privacy hold.** Author parts from published dimensions. Vendor CAD (for example the Raspberry Pi STEP in the ignored `digital-twin/cad/source-vault/`) and the owner's private photographs are cross-check references only. They never enter tracked files, Studio packs or app bundles, and scratch copies and overlays are deleted after use.

## Standing rules

- Never weaken the M7 overlap checks in `closure.py` or `closure_verify.py`; a refused step stays refused until the geometry evidence changes.
- Preserve accepted milestone records, receipts and failures; changes are additive.
- The publication coordinator binds `package.json`, `vite.config.ts`, `src-tauri/tauri.conf.json` and `src-tauri/capabilities/default.json`. Changing one means archiving the prior generation and running `pnpm content:build` once with no dev server running (see `docs/implementation/M3_PUBLICATION_PLAN.md`).
